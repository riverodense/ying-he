Research

Research & Publications

Decision theory and decision analysis, risk and ambiguity, and applications to operations and business problems.

Research Areas

Decision Theory & Utility

Axiomatic foundations, utility and value theory, multiple-attribute preferences, intertemporal decision making, and decision analysis.

Decision Making under Ambiguity

Source-dependent ambiguity, two-stage evaluation models, recursive dynamic preferences, and ambiguity-sensitive decision making.

Operations & Business Applications

Pricing and revenue management, supply chains, data-driven operational analytics, preference learning, and project valuation.
