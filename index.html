About

My research lies at the intersection of decision theory, decision analysis, and operations management. One stream of my work develops axiomatic and analytical models of decision making, with particular interests in decision making under risk and ambiguity, intertemporal choice, recursive decision models, and multiattribute utility theory.

A second stream applies decision models to operations and business problems, including revenue management, pricing, supply chains, data-driven decision making, and project valuation. I received my Ph.D. from the McCombs School of Business at The University of Texas at Austin in 2013. My work has appeared in Management Science, Operations Research, Production and Operations Management, European Journal of Operational Research, Journal of Mathematical Economics, and other journals.

Research Areas

Decision Theory & Analysis

Utility and value theory, axiomatic models, multiattribute preferences, intertemporal choice, and decision analysis.

Risk & Ambiguity

Static and dynamic decision making under ambiguity, source-dependent uncertainty, recursive models, and real-option/project valuation.

Operations Applications

Revenue management, pricing, digital supply chains, data-driven operational analytics, and preference learning.
